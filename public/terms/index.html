5. Groups

  • Styles. A Family group shares one budget. A Split group records who paid and who shares each expense, keeps balances and suggests how to settle up. The style is chosen when the group is created and can't be changed.
  • Owning and joining. Every account has its own Personal space. With an active Pro+ plan you can own up to two groups: one Family group and one Split group. Anyone can also join one Family group and one Split group they're invited to, for free. Owning a group doesn't use up a join slot. That makes up to 3 spaces on Free or Personal (Personal plus 2 joined groups) and up to 5 on Pro+ (Personal, 2 owned and 2 joined). Each Pro+ group holds up to 12 people, including the Owner.
  • Roles. The Owner manages members, roles and the group itself. Admins can add and edit shared entries. Members have view-only access. Only the Owner or an Admin can record payments and member-paid expenses.
  • If the Owner's plan ends. The group pauses for everyone: it stays visible, but nothing new can be added until the Owner renews. In a Split group, payments can still be recorded so people can settle up.
  • Invitations. Only invite people you know who expect it. An invitation goes to the email you enter, and the person accepts with that account.
  • Leaving and deleting. Members can leave at any time. Their past entries stay in the group under their name. The Owner can delete the group: each member is first emailed a copy of the group's data, then the group is permanently deleted. Ownership can't be transferred.
  • Money between members is settled by you, outside RatePocket. RatePocket records what you enter and doesn't move money or guarantee that anyone pays.
  • Owners who created a group under an earlier plan keep that group under the terms shown in the app.

6. Rates, scans and accuracy

Exchange rates come from a third-party provider, can be delayed and may differ from your bank's. AI receipt scans can misread receipts, so check the result before you save it. Totals, budgets, balances and reports are only as accurate as the entries they're built from. "Recorded surplus" and "budget left" are calculations from your entries, not your bank balance.
