About RatePocket

Know what you can spend today, on your own or together.

Why it exists

Most budget apps tell you what you already spent. RatePocket starts from the other end: it takes your budget for the period and shows how much you can spend today, so everyday choices are easy. When money is shared, Family groups keep one household budget, and Split groups keep trips and shared flats fair without awkward maths.

What we care about

  • Your data stays yours. No bank login, ever. Without an account, everything stays on your phone.
  • Simple first. One number for today, entries in seconds, and reports that are easy to read.
  • Works anywhere. Offline logging, 150+ currencies and five languages, including Arabic and Urdu.
  • Fair pricing. The everyday budget is free. Plans add backup, no ads, more AI scans and groups.

Who makes it

RatePocket is made by Tradali, an independent app maker. It's available for iPhone and Android in English, Arabic, Spanish, French and Urdu.
