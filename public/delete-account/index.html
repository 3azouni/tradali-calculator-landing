RatePocket · Account & data deletion

Delete your RatePocket account & data

App: RatePocket · Package: com.tradali.calculator

RatePocket is operated by an individual developer using the name Tradali. Use this page to delete or request deletion of your RatePocket Cloud Backup account and associated cloud data. Google Play requires both an in-app deletion path and a web resource for apps that create accounts. RatePocket provides both.

What RatePocket accounts are

RatePocket does not use a separate email/password signup. An account is created only if you enable Cloud Backup, which requires an active subscription and Google Sign-In (via Supabase). Core calculator, converter, and local spending tracking work without an account.

Four different actions (do not confuse them)

  • Sign out — Settings → Sign out. Stops Cloud Backup sync for that session. Local spending data stays on the device. Your cloud account remains.
  • Clear data — Settings → Clear data. Removes spending history on this device, and also clears cloud spending data if you are signed in. Your Google sign-in and Play purchases stay.
  • Delete account — Settings → Delete account (signed-in only), or email request below. Permanently deletes your Cloud Backup authentication account and associated cloud spending data, then resets local spending data on that device.
  • Cancel Cloud Backup subscription — Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (or equivalent). Cancelling billing does not by itself delete your cloud account. Delete the account separately if you want cloud data removed.

In-app deletion (recommended when you still have the app)

  1. Open RatePocket on Android.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Account & data, make sure you are signed in with the Google account used for Cloud Backup.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

When you confirm, the app calls our authenticated deletion endpoint. That endpoint verifies your signed-in session, deletes cloud spending entries and periods linked to your user id, deletes the authentication account, then the app clears local spending data on the device. Cancel any Cloud Backup subscription in Google Play separately if you still have one.

What gets deleted for a full account deletion

  • Your Cloud Backup authentication account associated with RatePocket (Google/Supabase sign-in for this app).
  • Cloud-stored spending entries and spending periods linked to that account on our backend.
  • Local spending data on the device that confirmed in-app deletion (the app resets that device’s spending history).

We do not freeze or disable accounts as a substitute for deletion.

What is not removed automatically

  • Google Play purchase and subscription records retained by Google under Google Play policies. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play.
  • Advertising / device identifiers processed by Google Mobile Ads under Google’s ads systems. Uninstalling and resetting the advertising ID on your device are additional controls.
  • Receipt-scan quota counters keyed by on-device install identifier (not by Google account). These are not the same as a Cloud Backup account and are not automatically erased by account deletion. Ask support if you want related records reviewed.
  • OpenAI processing of receipt images you previously scanned — subject to OpenAI’s API retention practices (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Support email correspondence kept as needed for ordinary business records.

Web / email deletion (if you uninstalled the app)

Tap the button below to email support.tradali@gmail.com with a pre-filled deletion request. Include the Google account email you used to sign in for Cloud Backup so we can locate your data.

Request account deletion by email Contact support

What happens after you email us

  1. We verify the request (we may ask you to confirm ownership of the Google account used for Cloud Backup).
  2. We delete the associated RatePocket cloud account and linked cloud spending data from our systems.
  3. We reply to confirm completion. For email requests, we aim to complete deletion within 30 days after we can verify ownership (often sooner). In-app deletion for a signed-in user is processed immediately by the app’s deletion endpoint when the request succeeds.

Local-only users (no Cloud Backup sign-in)

If you never signed in, there is no cloud account to delete. To remove local RatePocket data from a device, use Settings → Clear data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.
