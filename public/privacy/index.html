Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026 · Applies to the RatePocket app for iPhone and Android (com.tradali.calculator) and this website.

The short version

  • No account needed. Without signing in, your budget and entries stay on your phone.
  • No bank connection. RatePocket never asks for bank logins. You decide what you record.
  • Cloud only when you choose it. Backup, sync and groups are stored in our database in the EU (Paris).
  • We don't sell your data and don't use your entries to target ads.
  • You're in control. Clear this device, clear your cloud backup, leave or delete a group, or delete your account. See Account & data deletion.

1. Who we are

RatePocket is made and published by an individual developer working under the name Tradali. Tradali is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: support.tradali@gmail.com. Brand site: tradali.com.

2. What we process

2.1 On your phone (no account)

Your budget, periods, categories, income sources, expenses and income (amounts, currencies, dates, titles, notes), preferences, and an install identifier used for receipt-scan limits. This stays on your device until you turn on backup or use a feature that sends data (described below).

2.2 Your account

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple, Google or email and password (with a one-time code sent by email). We receive your account ID and email address, and with Apple you can choose to hide your email. We use them to sign you in, keep your backup and groups linked to you, and handle purchases and support.

2.3 Cloud backup & sync

With a Personal or Pro+ plan and signed in, your personal entries, budget periods and preferences are stored in our database so you can restore and sync them on your other devices.

2.4 Groups (Family and Split)

When you create or join a group we store: the group's name, style (Family or Split), currency and time zone; members, roles (Owner, Admin, Member) and display names; invitations (the invited email address and status); the group's shared entries, budget and categories; for Split groups, who paid, each person's share, payments between members and trips; and an activity log (who added or changed what). Every member of a group can see that group's shared data, according to their role. Your Personal space is never shared with a group.

2.5 AI receipt scan

Scanning is optional and starts only when you choose Camera or Gallery. The receipt image goes through our server function straight to the OpenAI API, which reads the shop, date, currency and items. You review the result before anything is saved.

  • RatePocket never saves receipt images or screenshots, not in our database and not in any file storage. The image exists only for the moment it takes to read it.
  • We keep a scan counter per account (or per install, if you're not signed in) to apply your plan's limit: 2 trial scans on Free, 35 a month with Personal, 100 a month with Pro+.
  • OpenAI's API doesn't use the image to train its models. Under OpenAI's own API policy, OpenAI may keep request data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring before deleting it.

2.6 Purchases

Subscriptions and one-off purchases are made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. RevenueCat tells the app which plan you have. We receive purchase status and product, never your card details.

2.7 Ads (Free plan only)

On the Free plan the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Personal and Pro+ have no ads. Google may process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location from your IP address and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message first, and you can change your choice later in the app's ad privacy settings. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before using the advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency).

2.8 App integrity (Android)

On Android, some sensitive actions are checked with Google's Play Integrity API, which confirms the request comes from the genuine app on a real device.

2.9 Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token so we can tell you about group activity (for example, a new shared expense or a payment reminder). Push messages are delivered through Expo and Apple or Google's push services.

2.10 Emails

We send sign-in codes, group invitations and, when a group is deleted, a copy of that group's data to its members. These emails are sent through Resend.

2.11 Exchange rates

The app downloads currency rates from exchangerate.fun. This is a normal web request from your phone. It doesn't include your entries.

2.12 Support

If you email us, we process your message and anything you include in it.

7. Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access: get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct: fix inaccurate data. Most of it you can edit directly in the app.
  • Erase: delete your data. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us (see Account & data deletion).
  • Port: receive your data in a reusable format. The app exports PDF and CSV, and we can send your cloud data on request.
  • Restrict or object: limit or object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, for example for personalized ads or notifications, without affecting earlier processing.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

Email support.tradali@gmail.com from the address on your account, or tell us which sign-in you use. We may ask you to confirm the request comes from you, and we reply within one month. Requests are free.

8. Your controls in the app

  • Sign out in Settings. Your local records stay on this phone.
  • Clear this device, clear cloud backup, delete account: Settings → Data & privacy → Clear data & more.
  • Leave a group: in the group's Members screen. Delete a group (Owner only): in the group's settings.
  • Ads: change your consent in the app's ad privacy settings, or reset or limit the advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

9. Security

Data moves over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to cloud data is limited to your account and, for groups, to that group's members by database rules on our server. No system is perfect: protect your phone and sign-in, and only invite people you trust to a group.

10. Children

RatePocket isn't meant for children. Don't use it if you're under 13, or under the age your country requires to agree to data processing without a parent (up to 16 in some EU countries). If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. This website

This site doesn't use analytics, advertising cookies or trackers, and it loads its fonts from our own server. It's hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site and may set strictly necessary security cookies.

12. Changes and contact

We'll update this policy when the app or the law changes, and change the date at the top. Questions or requests: support.tradali@gmail.com.
