Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026 · App: RatePocket (Android package com.tradali.calculator) · Support: support.tradali@gmail.com

RatePocket is an Android calculator, currency converter, and spending tracker. Most personal features are local-first and work without an account. This policy describes what the app and related website process — including optional Google Cloud Backup, optional Family / Group shared workspaces, Google Play purchases (via RevenueCat), advertising (AdMob), and optional AI receipt scanning.

Not all data stays only on your device. Advertising, purchases, optional Cloud Backup, optional Family / Group, optional receipt scans, invite emails, and support emails involve network transmission as described below.

1. Who we are

RatePocket is operated by an individual developer using the name Tradali. Tradali is a developer/publishing name, not a registered company. Product site: ratepocket.tradali.com. Brand site: tradali.com. Contact: support.tradali@gmail.com.

For privacy or deletion requests, email the address above. We will use that channel to identify your request and respond.

2. Information we process

2.1 On-device app data (local)

Stored on your device (for example via on-device storage) and used to run the app:

  • Calculator / converter preferences (amounts, selected currencies)
  • Salary/budget, income label, pay period dates, spending entries (amounts, currencies, dates/times, titles/notes, categories), category allocations, and appearance preference
  • Export entitlement flags, export credits, and similar preference flags stored locally
  • An install identifier used to enforce receipt-scan quotas (see below)

Local personal spending data stays on the device unless you enable Cloud Backup or use features that upload content (receipt scan). Family / Group shared ledgers are stored on our backend when you create or join a Family workspace.

2.2 Optional Google account (Cloud Backup and Family / Group)

Google sign-in is not required for calculator, conversion, or local personal spending. Cloud Backup and Family / Group require Google sign-in (via Supabase Auth / Google OAuth). We receive account identifiers Google provides for that sign-in (such as Google user id and associated email) so we can authenticate you, associate Cloud Backup data with your account, and manage Family membership and invites.

2.3 Cloud Backup content

When Cloud Backup is active and you are signed in, personal spending entries and spending periods may be stored on our backend (Supabase), linked to your authenticated user id. Fields can include amounts, currencies, dates/times, titles, categories, notes, and period settings.

2.3a Family / Group content

When you create or join a Family / Group workspace, we store workspace metadata (name, currency, time zone, seat limits), membership and roles (Owner, Admin, Member), invitation emails and invitation status, shared spending entries and period settings for that workspace, attribution (who added or last edited an entry), and an activity / audit log of Family events. Personal workspace data is never mixed into a Family ledger. Invite emails are sent to the address the Owner provides so the invitee can accept with the matching Google account.

2.4 Purchases and entitlements

In-app purchases and subscriptions are sold through Google Play Billing. The app uses RevenueCat to read purchase/entitlement status (for example Lifetime Export Pass, Cloud Backup, and Family / Group). We receive entitlement status needed to unlock features. We do not receive your full payment card details. Google processes payments under Google’s terms.

2.5 Advertising (AdMob)

The free experience may show ads via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), including banner, native, rewarded, rewarded interstitial, and app-open formats where enabled. Google may process device and advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID where available), IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, and diagnostic data under Google’s policies to serve and measure ads.

Where required (for example EEA/UK/Switzerland), the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent / privacy choices before personalized ad requests. You may reopen privacy options from Settings → Ad privacy options when the SDK indicates that an entry point is required for your region.

2.6 Optional AI receipt scanning

If you choose Camera or Gallery scan when adding a spend, the app first shows a disclosure. If you cancel, no image is uploaded. If you continue, the selected receipt image is sent to our Supabase Edge Function, which forwards the image to OpenAI vision models to extract suggested line items (merchant/date/currency/items). You review and edit results before saving spends.

  • What we send for a scan: the selected image and its MIME type, plus an install identifier and a Cloud Backup entitlement flag used only to choose the correct scan quota.
  • What RatePocket stores on our backend after a scan: a scan-count record keyed by install identifier and quota period (lifetime free bucket, or calendar month for Cloud Backup). We do not keep a permanent gallery of receipt photos on our servers. The image is handled for that request; our code does not write receipt images into a database or object store.
  • What OpenAI receives: the image content as part of the analysis request. Under OpenAI’s current API data controls, API inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI models by default (unless an organization opts in). OpenAI may retain content for abuse monitoring for up to 30 days unless a customer has approved Zero Data Retention or similar controls. RatePocket does not claim Zero Data Retention for this integration. See OpenAI’s documentation: Data controls in the OpenAI platform and API data usage policies.
  • Quotas (current product): free installs — 3 lifetime scans; Cloud Backup subscribers — 75 scans per calendar month.

2.7 Exchange rates

The app fetches currency rate data from network rate providers so conversions can update. This involves ordinary network requests from your device; it is not Cloud Backup.

2.8 Diagnostics and support

If you email support, we process the content of your message and any account details you include. Billing/SDK logs may appear on your device or in developer tooling during troubleshooting; we do not operate a separate analytics product beyond what AdMob, RevenueCat, Supabase, and OpenAI provide for their services.

3. How we use information

  • Provide calculator, conversion, spending tracking, export, Cloud Backup, Family / Group, and optional receipt-scan features
  • Authenticate optional Cloud Backup and Family / Group with Google; sync personal backups and shared Family ledgers across devices you use with the same account
  • Send Family invitation emails and enforce seat / workspace caps
  • Unlock paid features you purchase through Google Play
  • Show ads and honor consent / privacy choices where required
  • Enforce receipt-scan quotas and respond to support / deletion requests

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your Cloud Backup or Family spending content to personalize ads. Other members of a Family workspace can see shared ledger entries and activity for that workspace according to their role.

4. Sharing / service providers

We use processors or independent platforms as needed to run the product:

  • Google — Google Sign-In (optional Cloud Backup and Family / Group), Google Play Billing, AdMob / UMP
  • RevenueCat — purchase/entitlement status bridging Google Play purchases to the app
  • Supabase — authentication session backend, Cloud Backup storage, Family / Group workspace data, invite email edge function, receipt-scan edge function and quota counters
  • Resend (or similar email provider) — delivery of Family invitation emails when an Owner sends an invite
  • OpenAI — optional receipt image analysis when you start a scan

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. Provider policies may change; the links in this policy are for their current published documentation.

5. Retention

  • Local data — until you clear app storage, use in-app clear/delete controls, or uninstall (subject to OS behavior).
  • Cloud Backup data — while your Cloud Backup account/data exists, or until you delete the account in-app, request deletion via our Delete account page, or email support.
  • Family / Group data — while the workspace exists and you remain a member. Leaving a Family removes your membership; deleting a Family (Owner) removes that workspace’s shared ledger and related records as implemented in the product. Account deletion removes your authentication account and associated memberships / owned Family data as described on the Delete account page.
  • Receipt images — transmitted for the scan request as described above. RatePocket does not operate a permanent receipt photo library. OpenAI’s retention for API abuse monitoring (and any Zero Data Retention eligibility) is governed by OpenAI’s terms, not by a RatePocket-controlled gallery.
  • Install identifiers and scan-quota counters — the install id is stored on the device for as long as app storage remains. Matching quota counters on our backend remain so limits can be enforced. Account deletion removes Cloud Backup account data; it does not automatically erase install-id quota counters, because those counters are tied to the device install id rather than the Google account. You may ask support to review related records when you request deletion.
  • Support emails — as needed to handle your request and ordinary business records.
  • Google / RevenueCat — subject to those providers’ retention practices for their services.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • Use RatePocket without creating an account.
  • Decline optional receipt scans; enter spends manually.
  • Sign out in Settings — local data remains on the device; Cloud Backup and Family sync stop for that session.
  • Clear data in Settings — removes spending history on the device, and also clears cloud personal spending data if you are signed in; purchases and sign-in stay. Family memberships may need Leave / Delete Family actions in the app.
  • Leave or delete a Family in Family settings — Leave removes you from that workspace; Delete Family (Owner only) removes the shared workspace after confirmation.
  • Delete account in Settings (when signed in) — permanently deletes your authentication account and associated cloud personal spending data, then resets local spending data on that device. Family memberships and owned Groups are cleaned up as described on the Delete account page. You can also request deletion by email from that page.
  • Manage or cancel Play subscriptions (Cloud Backup and/or Family / Group) in Google Play subscription settings (separate from account deletion).
  • Where shown, use Ad privacy options / system advertising settings applicable to your region.

7. Security

Network features (Cloud Backup, Family / Group, receipt scan, rates, billing entitlement checks, invite emails) use HTTPS in transit. No security measure is perfect; protect your device and Google account. Only invite people you trust into a Family workspace.

8. Children

RatePocket is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International processing

Providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate mechanisms offered by those providers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy where permitted by law.
