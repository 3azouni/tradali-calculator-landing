Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026 · App: RatePocket (Android package com.tradali.calculator) · Developer: Tradali

RatePocket is built to work local-first. Most features work without an account. Optional sign-in with your Google account is only used for Cloud Backup. Advertising and purchases use Google services as described below.

1. Who we are

RatePocket is published by Tradali. Contact: support.tradali@gmail.com. Studio site: tradali.com. Product site: ratepocket.tradali.com.

2. Information we process

  • On-device app data — calculator state, selected currencies, salary/budget, pay period, spending entries, export entitlement flags, and similar preferences stored locally on your device (for example via on-device storage). This data stays on your device unless you enable Cloud Backup.
  • Optional account (Cloud Backup) — if you choose Cloud Backup, you sign in directly with your Google account. We receive the account identifiers Google provides for that sign-in (such as your Google user ID and the email associated with it) so we can identify your backup and keep it synced across your devices.
  • Cloud backup content — when Cloud Backup is enabled, spending entries and spending periods you create may be stored on our backend (Supabase) and linked to your signed-in user id. Fields can include amounts, currencies, dates/times, titles, categories, notes, and period settings.
  • Purchases — Lifetime Export Pass and Cloud Backup subscriptions are processed by the platform billing system (Google Play). We receive purchase/entitlement status needed to unlock features; we do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Advertising — the free experience may show ads via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect device and advertising identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID where available), approximate location derived from IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic data under Google’s policies to serve and measure ads.
  • Diagnostics / support — if you email us, we process the content of your message and any account details you include so we can help you.

3. How we use information

  • To provide calculator, currency conversion, spending tracking, export, and Cloud Backup features.
  • To authenticate optional Cloud Backup sign-in with Google and sync your data across devices you use with the same account.
  • To unlock paid features you purchase.
  • To show ads in the free experience (where enabled).
  • To respond to support and account/data deletion requests.

We do not use your Google account identifiers or Cloud Backup content to serve or personalize ads.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers that process data on our behalf or as independent controllers for their services:

  • Google — Google Sign-In (when you choose it), Google Play Billing, and Google Mobile Ads / AdMob.
  • Supabase — backend storage for your Cloud Backup content (spending entries and periods), keyed to your Google-authenticated user id.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Retention

  • Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall the app, or otherwise delete it on-device.
  • Cloud Backup data is retained while your Cloud Backup account/data exists, or until you request deletion.
  • Support emails are kept as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business records.

6. Your choices and deletion

  • You can use RatePocket without creating an account.
  • You can sign out of Cloud Backup in the app; local data remains on the device.
  • You can request deletion of your Cloud Backup account and associated cloud data. See our Account & data deletion page, or email support.tradali@gmail.com. Freezing an account is not used as a substitute for deletion.
  • Uninstalling the app removes local on-device app data from that device (subject to your OS behavior).

7. Security

We handle personal and sensitive user data securely. Cloud Backup and related network traffic use modern cryptography in transit (HTTPS).

8. Children

RatePocket is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. International processing

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards provided by those providers.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy or data: support.tradali@gmail.com
