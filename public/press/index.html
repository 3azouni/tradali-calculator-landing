One line

RatePocket shows you how much you can spend today, on your own or together with family and friends.

Short description

RatePocket is a budget app that turns your budget into one daily number: what you can spend today. Log spending in seconds or scan a receipt with AI, convert 150+ currencies, and see clear reports. Family groups share one household budget, and Split groups track who paid and who owes on trips and in shared flats, then settle up in the fewest payments. No bank login, works offline, in five languages.
