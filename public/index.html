WHAT RATEPOCKET DOES

Not only where money went — how much you can spend today.

RatePocket is operated by an individual developer using the name Tradali. Use it on Android without creating an account for personal tracking. Cloud Backup and Family / Group require Google sign-in and an active paid plan where applicable.

Today Available

See a clear daily allowance from your period budget, with carryover from unused days so you stay on plan without guessing.

Category budgets

Allocate your period budget across categories, track remaining and overspend, and keep “available to allocate” honest as you adjust.

Family / Group

Share one ledger with up to 8 seats. Invite by email, set Owner / Admin / Member roles, and keep Personal spending private.

Calculator & currency conversion

Do everyday math and convert between currencies with live rates. Search by currency code or country name, then log a spend or income.

History, reports & exports

Review activity, daily budget ledgers, and reports. Export PDF or CSV via a rewarded ad (limited), a $0.99 credit, Lifetime Pass, or Cloud Backup while subscribed.

Optional Cloud Backup

Paid monthly or yearly Google Play subscription with Google sign-in. Back up and sync personal spending, restore on a new phone, unlock unlimited exports, and raise the category limit to 14. Ads remain on.

Install from Google Play: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tradali.calculator. Opening this website does not require a Play login.
Yes for personal core features — Today Available, calculator, converter, and category tracking are free to use, with ads. Free installs include up to 4 categories. Exporting as PDF/CSV can use a limited free rewarded-ad unlock, a $0.99 export credit, the one-time $6.99 Lifetime Export Pass, or Cloud Backup while subscribed. Cloud Backup and Family / Group are separate Google Play subscriptions and use Google sign-in.
Today Available is your daily spending allowance from the period’s daily budget pool, including carryover from unused earlier days when applicable. It works the same way in a Personal workspace and in a Family / Group workspace. It is a budget guide — not a bank balance or financial advice.
Family / Group is an optional shared workspace with its own budget, categories, expenses, and income. The Owner subscribes via Google Play ($7.99/month or $76.99/year), invites people by email, and assigns roles. Personal spending never mixes into a Family ledger. Each account may join or create up to 3 Family workspaces, and own at most 2 of them.
The Owner sends an invitation to a Google email. The invitee opens the link (or the in-app deep link), signs in with that same Google account, and accepts. If they are signed in with a different email, RatePocket shows an account check and asks them to switch. Invitations expire; cancelled invites cannot be reused.
Cloud Backup is an optional Google Play subscription ($1.99/month or $14.99/year) for personal data. It requires an active subscription and Google sign-in. While both are in place, RatePocket can back up and sync personal spending history, restore on a new phone, unlock unlimited exports, and raise the category limit to 14. Family / Group sync is separate and requires the Family plan. Ads stay on. Cancel anytime in Google Play.
When adding a spend, you can scan a bill with the camera or pick a photo from your gallery. RatePocket uploads the image to our server, which uses an AI vision model (OpenAI) to suggest line items you can edit before saving. Free installs get 3 lifetime scans. Cloud Backup subscribers get 75 scans per calendar month. See the Privacy Policy.
Core features work without an account. If you used Cloud Backup or Family / Group, open Settings → Account & data → Delete account while signed in, or use the Delete account page / email support.tradali@gmail.com. Leaving or deleting a Family workspace is separate from cancelling a Play subscription — see that page.
