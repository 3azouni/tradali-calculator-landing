FAQ

Quick answers

When can I download it?

Soon, on the App Store and Google Play. The buttons here will link to each store the day it's live.

Is it really free?

Yes. Budget, income, reports and the converter are free, with ads. Plans add backup, no ads, more scans and groups.

Family or Split: what's the difference?

Family shares one budget. Split tracks who paid and who owes, then helps you settle up. You pick the style when you create the group.

Can my group see my personal spending?

No. Your Personal space is never shared. Groups only see what's added to the group.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. You add entries yourself or scan a receipt. No bank login, ever.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Data & privacy → Clear data & more. Details on the deletion page.
