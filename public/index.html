WHAT RATEPOCKET DOES

Clear tools for everyday money.

RatePocket is operated by an individual developer using the name Tradali. Use it on Android without creating an account. Cloud Backup requires an active subscription and Google sign-in; calculator, convert, and local spending do not.

Calculator & currency conversion

Do everyday math and convert between currencies with live rates. Search by currency code or country name.

Spending, budgets & history

Log expenses with categories (spending groups), set a salary or budget for a month or custom period, and review balance, charts, and past periods on your device.

PDF & CSV exports

Export spending as PDF or CSV. Unlock options: watch a rewarded ad (limited free unlock), pay a one-time export credit, buy the Lifetime Export Pass, or use Cloud Backup while subscribed.

Optional Cloud Backup

Paid monthly or yearly Google Play subscription. Cloud Backup requires an active subscription and Google sign-in. While both are in place, RatePocket can back up and sync spending history, restore on a new phone, and unlock unlimited exports plus a higher group limit. Ads remain on.

Optional AI receipt scan

When adding a spend, you can photograph a receipt or pick one from your gallery. The image is sent to our server for AI extraction so you can review line items before saving. Free installs get a small lifetime scan quota; Cloud Backup subscribers get a monthly quota. Images are processed for that scan — see the Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The free experience may show Google AdMob ads (banner, native, rewarded, and related formats). Paid export unlocks do not remove ads.
